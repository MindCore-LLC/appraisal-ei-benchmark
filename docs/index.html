Public metrics

v2.0.0 public metrics are calibration and discriminant validity only. Roadmap slots (value–action, persistence, acoustic risk, steering) are named and null — they are not zeros and they are not averaged into a headline. Human mimicry is measured (reported, not averaged): can a discriminator tell the model’s vectors from rater vectors? 1.0 = indistinguishable from the rater pool. The human reference band — the same test run with each real rater as the “model” — is 0.08–0.46, because every rater has a fingerprint; all three measured models (0.15–0.18) sit inside that band. Discriminant validity near 0.25 means models recover “this is bad” better than they separate unfair vs uncontrollable vs urgent.
